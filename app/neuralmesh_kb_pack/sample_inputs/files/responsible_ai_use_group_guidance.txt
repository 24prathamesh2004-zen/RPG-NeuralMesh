Responsible AI use, group guidance note

Guidance for every company of the group, issued in October 2026. An AI assistant is used only through the approved gateways of the company. No confidential data of a customer, a supplier or an employee is put into a prompt. A person reads and approves every output before it is used in work that leaves the team.

What is allowed

Drafting code, tests and documents with an AI assistant.
Summarising a document that the person may read under the sharing rule of its catalogue item.
Asking an AI assistant for the first pass of a code review. A person still approves the change.

What is not allowed

Putting commercial terms, a product recipe, a salary or a contract with a customer into a prompt.
Using an AI tool that the company has not approved, also for a short trial.
Sending an output to a customer when no person has read it.

What each team keeps

The name of the person who approved each output that left the team.
A count of the errors that this person found in the outputs, month by month.

Who to ask

The information security lead of the company decides whether a tool is approved. The data protection lead decides whether a kind of data may be put into a prompt.

This note is guidance. The AI policy of each company stays the binding rule.
