# Jointing procedure check sheet, 33 kV cable joints, revision 3

The installer ticks each line of this sheet on the digital job card while the joint is made. Revision 3 adds two lines after six failed joints were cut open: stop work when rain or dust reaches the open cable, and take a photo of each sealed sleeve end.

## Before the cable is opened

- The kit batch on the label is the batch on the digital job card.
- A tent or a cover stands over the joint bay.
- The tools are clean, and the crimp tool is inside its calibration date.

## While the joint is made

1. Strip the cable ends to the lengths marked on the kit sheet.
2. Clean and degrease the insulation with the approved wipes.
3. Crimp the connector and check it for gaps.
4. Place the shrink sleeve over the connector and heat it evenly from the centre outwards.
5. Seal both sleeve ends and take a photo of each end.

Stop work when rain or dust reaches the open cable. Cover the cable, and start again from step 2.

## Before the circuit is energised

- The insulation resistance test is done, and the reading is on the job card.
- The second engineer has looked at the photos and the readings and has signed off the job record.
