Teardown findings, failed 33 kV cable joints, July to September 2026

Failure analysis by the field service team of Raychem RPG, November 2026.

Six failed joints were cut open and checked for the four causes in the set order. Water entry was the cause in 3 joints, a preparation error in 2 and a connector defect in 1. No joint failed from overload. All 3 joints with water entry were installed in rain, and the work was not stopped when rain reached the open cable.

How the teardown was done

Each failed joint stayed in place until photos were taken. It was then cut open along its length, and the point where the fault path started was found. The four causes were checked in order: preparation error, water entry, connector defect and overload of the circuit. This failed joint teardown followed the failure analysis procedure of the field service team.

What the job records showed

5 of the 6 joints had a digital job card. 3 of these 5 had no photo of the sealed sleeve end, and these 3 were the joints with water entry. The second engineer had signed off all 5 records. The sign-off looked at the test readings and not at the photos.
