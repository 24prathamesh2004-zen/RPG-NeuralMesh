A service visit starts with the permit to work and a safety briefing. The engineer inspects each accessory for tracking, heat marks, cracked sleeves and loose lugs. Thermal readings are taken under load and compared with the previous visit. Findings are closed on the job card with photos. A defect that affects safety is reported to the control room before the engineer leaves the site.
