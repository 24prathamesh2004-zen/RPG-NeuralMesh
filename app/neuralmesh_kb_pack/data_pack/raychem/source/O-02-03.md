Every installation has a digital job record. It holds the kit batch, the test readings and photos of each step. A second engineer reviews the record and signs it off before the circuit is handed over. Records with missing readings are returned to the installer within 2 working days. Monthly, the quality team counts returned records per installer team and shares the trend.
