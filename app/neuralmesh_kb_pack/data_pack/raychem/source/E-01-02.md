Cold-shrink terminations are placed over the prepared cable and released by pulling out the removable core. They need no flame and suit confined spaces. Heat-shrink terminations are shrunk with a torch and suit outdoor work. Both types need a clean stress-control layer. The lug end is sealed with mastic. The installer records the batch number of the kit.
