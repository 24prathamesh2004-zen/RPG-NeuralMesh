The failed joint is kept in place until photos are taken. It is then cut open along its length. The team finds where the breakdown path started. Four causes are checked in order, namely preparation errors, water entry, a connector defect and overload of the circuit. The finding is filed with the cause. Where the cause is a preparation error, the installer team gets a refresher session.
