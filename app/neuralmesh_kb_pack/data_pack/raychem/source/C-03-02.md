High voltage wiring in electric vehicles uses heat-shrink sleeves with an adhesive lining. They seal harness splices and the backs of connectors. Selection depends on the temperature class near the battery and on the bend radius of the route. Orange sleeves mark high voltage runs. A sample splice is cut open at each batch to check that the lining has flowed.
