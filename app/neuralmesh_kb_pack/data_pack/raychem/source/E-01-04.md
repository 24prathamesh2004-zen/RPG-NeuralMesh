At a transformer or switchgear bushing, the installer first checks the phase marking against the single line diagram. The termination is fitted to the bushing with the correct stress cone. The lug is tightened to the set torque and marked with a paint line. Earth continuity is checked before the cover is closed. Clearances to the cover are measured and written in the job card.
