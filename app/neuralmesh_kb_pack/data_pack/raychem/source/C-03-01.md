Charging stations need sealed accessories rated for outdoor use. The cables see repeated plugging, vibration and water spray. The note lists how to choose a sealed joint or termination by cable size, voltage level and exposure to water. Cold-shrink kits are preferred in tight cabinets. The note advises a test reading at every accessory before the station opens to users.
