A joint is installed in five steps. First the cable ends are stripped to the lengths marked on the kit sheet. The insulation is then cleaned and degreased with the approved wipes. The connector is crimped and checked for gaps. The shrink sleeve is placed over the connector and heated evenly from the centre outwards. Work stops if rain or dust reaches the open cable.
