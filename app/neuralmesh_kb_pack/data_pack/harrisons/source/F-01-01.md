Collection rounds start at first light. Cups are emptied within the agreed window after tapping. Latex is poured through a clean strainer into rinsed buckets. Buckets are covered and kept in shade. The collector logs the time of collection at the collection point.
Cups and buckets are rinsed daily; visible coagulum is kept apart from liquid latex and never mixed back.
