Report the median and the range beside the average. Any value more than 3 times the median of its group is flagged. The client is asked about a flagged value before it is removed. A change is called a trend only when it shows in at least 3 months in a row. Each month is compared with the same month of the previous year. Charts show the unit on the axis and start at zero for bars.
