Multiply each activity amount by one emission factor for the same fuel or energy type. The factor source and year are written beside every line of the workbook. Units are converted before multiplying, for example litres of diesel to kilograms. When a factor is replaced, the old and new totals are shown side by side. A reviewer re-calculates 5 lines of every 50.
