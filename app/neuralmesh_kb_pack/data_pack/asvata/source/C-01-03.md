Each engagement has a plan with 4 to 8 milestones. Each milestone has an owner and a date. The coordinator sends a status every week with three lists: done, next and blocked. A blocked item older than 5 working days goes to the delivery head. Dates that move are changed in the plan and the reason is written next to the change.
