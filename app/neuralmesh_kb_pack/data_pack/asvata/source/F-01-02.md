The shared calendar lists each client filing date and an internal date 10 working days before it. Reminders go to the analyst and the coordinator at 20, 10 and 3 working days before the internal date. A missed internal date is reported to the delivery head the same day. The coordinator updates the calendar when a client changes a filing date.
