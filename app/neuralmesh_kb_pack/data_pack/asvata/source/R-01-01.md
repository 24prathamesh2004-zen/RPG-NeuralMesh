Fix the boundary before any data is collected. List the sites and activities in scope and sign the list off with the client. Sort each activity into direct fuel use, purchased energy or other indirect sources. Record every exclusion with a reason. Keep the boundary the same from year to year, or restate the earlier years when it changes.
