Finished tyres are graded at the plant on four visual grades. Grade A: no visible bulge, cut or crack on sidewall, tread, shoulder or bead. Grade B: at most one surface cut under 10 mm, no bulge, no crack. Grade C: surface cuts and uneven wear marks allowed, no bulge, no crack. Reject: any bulge, any crack, or any bead damage, whatever its size. A bulge is graded Reject because it shows a separation inside the casing that a surface repair cannot reach. The grade and the defects found are recorded against the serial barcode.
