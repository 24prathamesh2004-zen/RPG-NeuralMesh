Every step of a batch is signed in the batch record at the time it is done. The entry shows the date, the time and the equipment used. Entries are never overwritten or erased. A correction is a single line through the entry with initials, date and reason. The supervisor checks the record before the batch moves to the next stage. Blank fields are marked as not applicable, never left empty.
