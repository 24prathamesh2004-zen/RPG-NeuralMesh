New representatives complete product training and pass a knowledge test before the first visit. Refresher tests run every 6 months. Samples are issued against a signed request and stored as labelled. They are counted at each visit and reconciled monthly. Expired samples are returned and never given out. Differences are reported to the area manager the same week.
