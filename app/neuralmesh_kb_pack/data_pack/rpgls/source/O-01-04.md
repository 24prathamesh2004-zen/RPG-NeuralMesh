Any unsafe event without injury is reported on a short form before the end of the shift. Anyone may report without fear of blame. The plant safety group reviews all forms weekly. Reports are grouped by area and cause. Each action is tracked to closure with an owner and a date. Three or more reports of the same kind in a month trigger a joint review with the area supervisors.
