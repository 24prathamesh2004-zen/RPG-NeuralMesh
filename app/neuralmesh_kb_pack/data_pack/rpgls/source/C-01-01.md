Representatives promote only within the approved product information. Facts are balanced and accurate. No gift or inducement is offered to prescribers. Claims outside the approved information are not made. Questions that go beyond the approved information are passed to the medical team. Any complaint about promotion is reported to the compliance lead within 2 working days.
