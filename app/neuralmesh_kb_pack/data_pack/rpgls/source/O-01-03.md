Solvents are drawn only in ventilated areas. Containers are earthed during transfer and no ignition source is allowed nearby. Hot work, confined space entry and maintenance in solvent areas need a signed work permit. The permit lists the hazards, the controls and the person who closes it. Operators wear the protective equipment listed at the area entrance. Spills are contained with absorbent material and reported the same shift.
