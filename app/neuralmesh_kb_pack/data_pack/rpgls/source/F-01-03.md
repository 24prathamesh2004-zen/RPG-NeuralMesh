A dossier is built from a checklist of sections, each owned by a named function. Each section is checked against source data by a second reviewer. Regulatory affairs runs a final consistency review of names, strengths and figures before submission. Questions raised by the authority are logged with the reply date. Answers are drafted by the owning function and approved by regulatory affairs.
