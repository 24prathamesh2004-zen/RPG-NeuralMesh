Every change to a process, material, site or specification is raised as a change request. Quality, production and regulatory affairs assess it together. Regulatory affairs decides whether the change needs a notification or prior approval. The change is implemented only after that decision is filed. Affected documents are updated and staff are trained before the first batch under the change.
