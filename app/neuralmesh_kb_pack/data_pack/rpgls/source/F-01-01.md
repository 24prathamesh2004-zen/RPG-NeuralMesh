A batch is released only after quality assurance reviews the batch record, the test results and every deviation. Each deviation is classed as minor, major or critical. It is investigated to root cause with the people who were on the line. A corrective and preventive action (CAPA) has an owner and a due date. Its effect is checked after 3 months. A repeat deviation reopens the earlier CAPA.
