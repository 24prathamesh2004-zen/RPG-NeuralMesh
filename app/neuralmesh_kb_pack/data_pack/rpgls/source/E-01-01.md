Samples are drawn by a written plan at defined stages of each batch. Tests use calibrated instruments and qualified analysts. A result outside its limit is a suspected out-of-specification result. The first step is a documented check for laboratory error. If none is found, a formal investigation opens within 1 working day. Retesting without a documented reason is not allowed. All raw data stays with the test record.
