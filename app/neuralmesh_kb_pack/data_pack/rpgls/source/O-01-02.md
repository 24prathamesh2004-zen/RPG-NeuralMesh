Before a new batch starts, the line is cleared of the previous product, labels, packing material and records. A second person verifies the clearance and signs. Equipment is cleaned by the written method for its type. A tag shows the cleaned status, the date and the hold time. If the hold time is exceeded, the equipment is cleaned again before use. Any doubt stops the line until the supervisor decides.
