A patrol grades each tower member on three priorities. P1: a bent member or a crack at a joint, made safe within 48 hours. P2: zinc loss with rust on more than a quarter of the member length, repair scheduled within the quarter. P3: surface rust with the zinc coat intact, recorded and checked again at the next patrol. Missing or loose bolts raise the priority of the member by one step.
