# Mixer gearbox breakdown RCA, Plant 2, October 2026

## What happened
On 6 October 2026 the gearbox of internal mixer M-4 at Plant 2 stopped during the second shift. The line was down for 19 hours. This is the third gearbox breakdown on the M-4 line in 2026, so it is treated as a recurring pattern across sites, not a single event.

## Root cause analysis
- Oil analysis showed metal particles above the alarm limit 12 days before the breakdown, and the reading was not acted on.
- The lubrication interval was set for a load of 80% and the mixer ran at 95% load since August 2026.
- The same bearing wear appeared in the breakdowns of February and June 2026.
