# Bale storage humidity check, Plant 3

Synthetic test file for NeuralMesh. CEAT raw material team, October 2026.

The check covered raw material storage in the covered bay during the last monsoon weeks. Humidity in the bay stayed above 80 percent on nine days. Bales stored for more than sixty days showed early signs of material ageing at the cut face.

- Lot acceptance at receipt now includes a moisture reading for every lot.
- Contamination and moisture control: pallets are lifted 15 cm off the floor and covered with breathable sheets.
- The time-to-use limit for lots received in the monsoon is cut from ninety to seventy days.

Next check in January 2027.
