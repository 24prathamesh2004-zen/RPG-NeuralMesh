# Knowledge transfer and handover playbook

Used when a delivery team takes over an application from another team, a client or an acquired unit.

## Weeks 1 and 2: shadowing
- The incoming team shadows the outgoing team on live tickets.
- Every runbook, password vault entry and deployment step is listed in one handover register.

## Weeks 3 and 4: reverse shadowing
- The incoming team handles tickets and the outgoing team watches.
- Open questions go to the handover register with an owner and a date.

## Week 5: sign-off
- The handover is signed off only when the register has no open item older than 5 working days.
- New joiners of the incoming team follow the onboarding checklist of the delivery unit before they get production access.

## Lessons from 2026 transitions
| Transition | Weeks planned | Weeks taken | Main lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance claims portal | 5 | 7 | Start the access requests in week 1 |
| Retail order platform | 5 | 5 | The handover register kept the scope clear |
| Acquired unit service desk | 6 | 9 | Agree process ownership before the systems cut-over |
