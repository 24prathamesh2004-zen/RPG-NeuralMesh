How NeuralMesh Works

Each group company keeps its own knowledge on its own node. A central graph knows which company holds what. A question goes only to the nodes that hold an answer, each node decides what may leave, and the result comes back as evidence records with citations, for the Ask page and for other applications such as SkillForge.

4company nodes: CEAT, KEC International, Zensar, Harrisons Malayalam (ports 8101 to 8104)
19knowledge packs across the four nodes, each checked against the pack schema
149entities in the central graph: 47 documents, 37 concepts, 29 datapoints, 14 experts, 8 domains, 7 skills, 4 companies, 3 roles
7steps for every question, from finding the concepts to the checked answer

In one line

Input is knowledge packs that follow the pack contract. The domain graph is the router in the middle. Output is evidence records that follow the evidence contract. The graph decides where to ask, and the nodes decide what may leave.

Part 1. Building the mesh (before any question)

Runs when a company adds or changes its knowledge. Nothing here calls an answer model.

  1. B1

    Each company describes its knowledge as datapoints and documents

    A datapoint is one area of knowledge with an owner, a sensitivity (Internal, Confidential, Restricted), a ceiling on what may leave (metadata only, summary, cited chunks, live aggregates) and the purposes it may be used for. Documents sit under a datapoint and are tagged with group concepts and skills.

    ExampleHarrisons datapoint O-05 "Aggregated latex-spoilage and estate trends", Internal, ceiling summary, with document O-05-02 "Spoilage by collection-to-factory time band".
    neuralmesh_kb_pack/kb/<node>.yaml, kb/annotations.yaml
  2. B2

    The pack builder writes knowledge packs and validates them

    The builder turns the YAML into 19 knowledge packs and checks every pack against the pack JSON Schema, together with the group packs (the vocabulary of 37 concepts, the approved links, and the skills and talent packs).

    neuralmesh_kb_pack/kbgen.py, knowledge/packgen.py, knowledge/validator.py
  3. B3

    Each node builds its own search indexes

    A keyword index (SQLite FTS5 with word stemming, so "failures" finds "failure reports") and a vector index per pack (FAISS with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embedding model). Only text the node may release goes into the indexes.

    knowledge/vector_index.py, mcp_nodes/knowledge_view.py
  4. B4

    Each node starts from its registry entry and offers seven tools

    Tools over MCP: health, describe_capabilities, search, summarize, find_experts, request_access and export_graph. Every call passes the node's policy enforcement point first. A new node needs a registry entry and a pack, no code.

    config/nodes.yaml, python -m mcp_nodes <id>, mcp_nodes/server.py, mcp_nodes/policy.py
  5. B5

    The central graph is built from the group packs and the node exports

    The mesh calls export_graph on every node. Each node returns its datapoints, document titles, concept tags, roles and consented experts, never document text. The graph store saves them by ID: a second load updates in place, and an item a node no longer exports is marked retired, never deleted. A link between two companies counts only after both owners approve it.

    ExampleCEAT export: 36 entities and 109 relationships. KEC: 27 and 65. Zensar: 16 and 38. Harrisons: 18 and 47.
    services/graph_store.py (SQLite), services/mesh_graph.py (NetworkX), services/mesh_runtime.py

Part 2. Answering a question (7 steps)

One LangGraph flow: resolve, route, fan_out, scope, expand, build_answer, assemble. The person does not choose a node.

  1. 1

    Resolve: find the concepts in the question

    Email addresses and phone numbers are removed from the question first. Then the question is matched against concept and skill names and their aliases as whole phrases. When no phrase matches, it is compared by meaning with every concept, and a score of 0.45 or more counts. Company names in the question are noted.

    Example"latex going bad before it reaches the factory" names no concept, but matches the concept "Latex pre-coagulation" by meaning with a score of 0.50.
    services/mesh_graph.py resolve, services/context/pii.py
  2. 2

    Route: walk the graph from concepts to companies

    From each concept the graph follows approved RELATED_TO, BROADER and SAME_AS links one hop, then finds the documents that mention those concepts, their datapoints and the companies that hold them. When the question names a company, only that company is kept, plus another company whose document has an approved link to it. When no concept is found, a classifier step picks the companies.

    ExampleFrom "Latex pre-coagulation" the graph reaches 7 documents in three companies: Harrisons O-05-01, O-05-03, F-01-02 and F-01-05, CEAT E-02-01 and E-03-02, and KEC E-05-01. So CEAT, Harrisons and KEC are asked.
    services/mesh_graph.py route and _narrow, GET /api/v1/mesh/routing shows the result without asking
  3. 3

    Fan out: ask the routed nodes in parallel

    Each routed node receives a signed token for that node and the purpose. The node checks every datapoint: purpose allowed, ceiling, never-leave rule, consent and access grants. It searches with keyword and vector search, merges both lists with the routed concepts by reciprocal rank fusion, and returns evidence records. A withheld item comes back as a title with the decision and the rule, never its text. A node that is down makes the answer partial, not failed.

    ExampleCEAT's E-01 compound formulation records come back as "BLOCKED_BY_POLICY, rule R-NL-01" with no content.
    services/neuralmesh.py fan_out, mcp_nodes/server.py search, mcp_nodes/policy.py
  4. 4

    Scope: choose the documents for the answer

    Sentences that read like instructions to a model are removed from the evidence and the record is flagged. The top 12 documents are taken in turn from each node's own ranking, so one node cannot crowd out another. A re-rank step marks documents that do not fit the question. Those move to "also found" and are still shown. If the re-rank rejects all of them, the top three stay.

    services/context/scoping.py, services/context/records.py
  5. 5

    Expand: add the approved links between documents

    The graph adds approved CORROBORATES, CONFLICTS_WITH, ADDRESSES_GAP and SUPERSEDES links that touch the chosen documents. When a link points to a document of another node, that node is asked for its summary through its own policy. Links to a node that blocked the question are left out.

    services/mesh_graph.py expand, services/neuralmesh.py expand
  6. 6

    Build the answer: context, model, checks

    The context builder fits the evidence into 24,000 characters in a fixed order: question, routing explanation, links, aggregates, summaries, chunks, withheld items. What does not fit is counted in an omission note. The model policy picks the model for the step (group gateway, local or cloud), and no Confidential or Restricted record goes to a cloud model. The written answer must pass seven checks by code. A failed answer is not shown, and the reason is.

    Checks1 every sentence cites a document in the bundle, 2 no canary string, 3 no content of a withheld item, 4 a conflict is shown on both sides, 5 no removed instruction repeated, 6 no email address or phone number, 7 at most 10 sentences.
    services/context/builder.py, policy.py, answer.py, check.py, config/model_policy.json
  7. 7

    Assemble: return the result and write the audit

    The response holds the answer (or the reason there is none), the evidence records, the "also found" list, the withheld items, the links, the routing and the check results. Each model call is written to a hash-chained central audit with no question or answer text. Identical questions are served from the answer cache and the re-rank cache.

    services/neuralmesh.py, services/context/llm.py (audit), services/context/cache.py

Input, graph, output

PartWhat it is
InputKnowledge packs in the pack format, plus each node's graph export. Checked by the pack schema.
GraphThe domain graph: concepts, documents, datapoints, companies, skills, roles, experts and approved links. Used to route and to expand. Holds no document text.
OutputEvidence records, schema_version 1.0, plus the checked answer. The same shape for the Ask page and for SkillForge.

Who decides what

  • The graph decides which companies to ask.
  • Each node decides what may leave it, per datapoint.
  • Code checks every written answer before it is shown.
  • Owners of both companies approve a link between them.

Who uses the output

Ask page of NeuralMesh, for people of the group companies.

SkillForge, through six context tools on /api/v1/context and the MCP gateway on port 8100: role_context, skill_context, ask, learning_sources, find_mentors, vocabulary_export. SkillForge also sends its talent packs to /api/v1/context/talent-packs.

Search at the node

Keyword and vector

  • Keyword: SQLite FTS5 per node with Porter stemming and a stopword list. A question of stopwords only finds nothing.
  • Vector: FAISS inner-product index per pack, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 run through ONNX Runtime.
  • Both lists and the routed concepts are merged by reciprocal rank fusion with k = 60.

What the node checks per datapoint

  • The purpose of the question is in purposes_allowed (rule R-PURPOSE-01).
  • The ceiling: metadata only, summary, cited chunks or live aggregates.
  • Never-leave datapoints return the title only, with the rule.
  • Confidential bodies need an approved access grant. Experts need consent.

How a change reaches an answer

Each of these seven changes was made on the running stack, followed to an answer, and then undone. No change needs code.

ChangeWhat to doWhat happens
A new document at a companyAdd it to the company YAML, run the pack builder, restart that node, refresh the graphFound by keyword and by meaning, exported to the graph, routed and cited in an answer. When removed again, its graph entries are retired.
A new alias for a conceptAdd it to the group vocabulary pack, refresh the graphA phrase that matched nothing before routes to the right company. No node changes.
A new role from SkillForgeSkillForge posts its talent pack to /context/talent-packsrole_context returns the role with its skills at once. A role missing from a later export is retired and returns 404.
A link between two companiesPropose it in the links pack, both owners approve on the Governance screenIt counts only after both approve. A rejection stands. An approved link is added to answers by the expand step.
Access to a gated documentRequest access, the datapoint owner approvesThe body is released as cited chunks, and withheld again after the grant is revoked. The node audit records request, review and search, without the justification text.
A fifth company nodeAdd a registry entry in nodes.yaml and its pack, start it with python -m mcp_nodes <id>Routed and answered like the other four, and its never-leave rules apply. Removed from the registry, it is retired in the graph.
A node goes downNothingThe answer is partial, the node is marked unavailable, and evidence from the other nodes still comes back.
