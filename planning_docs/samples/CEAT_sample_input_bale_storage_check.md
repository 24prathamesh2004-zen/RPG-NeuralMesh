# Bale storage check at the Plant 2 raw material store

The store team checked the natural rubber bales received in October. Three lots were close to the maximum storage time of ninety days and were issued first, two bales with torn wrapping were set aside under the foreign matter control rule, and the per-lot trending sheet now holds the dirt content and volatile matter results of every lot.

## What was checked

Every lot went through incoming inspection at the gate: the delivery note, the wrapping and the moisture of the outer bales were looked at before the lot was accepted. Lot acceptance was recorded per lot, with the supplier code and the receipt date on the lot card, so lot traceability from the bale to the mixing batch is kept.

## What was found

The three old lots came from one supplier. They were issued first, as FIFO storage requires, and each got a storage ageing check before mixing. The dirt content and volatile matter results were added to the per-lot trending sheet, so a drift of this supplier shows before the material reaches the mixer.

The two bales with torn wrapping were set aside, the wrapping was replaced and the bales were marked for use within two weeks.

## What changes

- Material handling in the covered bay: bales stay on pallets, off the floor and out of sunlight, with the oldest lot nearest the issue door.
- The supplier scorecard of the supplier with the three old lots gets a note on delivery timing, and the next joint visit will look at the supplier's own storage.
- A lot that passes ninety days is reported to the store lead on the same day.
